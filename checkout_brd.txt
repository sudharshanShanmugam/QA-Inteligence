
Module: Checkout
Priority: Critical
Owner: Product Team

BR-001: Cart Management
- User must be able to add, update, remove items from cart
- Cart must persist for 24 hours for logged-in users
- Guest cart must merge with user cart on login

BR-002: Coupon System
- Only one coupon allowed per order
- Coupon must be validated at time of checkout initiation (not at application)
- Expired coupons must be rejected with user-friendly message
- Coupon discount must not reduce order below minimum order value of $10

BR-003: Payment Processing
- System must support: Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Wallet
- Payment timeout must not exceed 30 seconds
- On failure, order must transition back to CHECKOUT_PENDING (retry allowed)
- Double-charge prevention: idempotency key required on every payment call

BR-004: Order Confirmation
- Inventory must be reserved at CHECKOUT_PENDING (not at ORDER_PLACED)
- Confirmation email must be sent within 60 seconds of ORDER_CONFIRMED
- Order number must be generated deterministically (not UUID)

BR-005: Cancellation Policy
- Free cancellation within 30 minutes of ORDER_CONFIRMED
- After 30 minutes: cancellation fee applies
- If item is already shipped: cancellation not allowed, return flow triggered
